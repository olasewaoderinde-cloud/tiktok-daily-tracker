Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

TikTok Daily Tracker is a personal tool designed to retrieve authorized TikTok account and video information for the purpose of monitoring personal content performance.

Information We Access

With the user's authorization, the application may access TikTok account statistics such as follower count and information about the user's TikTok videos, including available video performance statistics.

How Information Is Used

The information is used solely to generate personal TikTok performance reports and track changes in video performance and follower growth.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share authorized TikTok information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information accessed through the application.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the application owner through the contact information associated with this project.
